## Signing

To sign this agreement, comment on your pull request:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The CLA bot records your signature; you only need to sign once.

## Why a CLA?

The Wispers hub is AGPL-licensed, and the same code also powers the hosted
Wispers Connect service under different terms. The CLA is what lets
contributions flow into both without asking every contributor again later.
Your code always remains available under the AGPL.
